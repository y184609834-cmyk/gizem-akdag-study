# Portrait

## Portrait goal

Keep the person recognizably real while allowing one part of the portrait to reorganize the world.

## Ask before generating

- What is the strongest bodily gesture?
- What clothing shape already has graphic potential?
- What hair movement can become spatial?
- What prop already has a relationship to the environment?
- What environmental texture can echo the body?

## Strong strategies

### Hair as spatial mass
Hair can become:
- landscape
- black field
- directional flow
- horizon interruption

### Garment as environment
A dress, sleeve, scarf, or coat can echo:
- flowers
- clouds
- water
- dunes
- architecture

### Body/environment fusion
Examples:
- vegetation shares texture with clothing
- terrain echoes shoulders or silhouette
- jewelry/ornament becomes environmental rhythm
- posture aligns with architecture

### Narrative suspension
Use:
- gaze
- gesture
- props
- repetition
- unexplained orientation

to imply an event without explaining it.

## Avoid

- replacing the person with a stylized stranger
- generic fashion glam retouch
- giant prop with no relationship
- decorative fantasy additions
- overloading the portrait with surreal symbols
