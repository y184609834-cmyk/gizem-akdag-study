# Evaluation

Score each axis 0–2.

## A. Source fidelity
0 — source identity mostly lost
1 — some anchors remain
2 — unmistakably derived from this exact photograph

## B. Relationship invention
0 — no relationship change
1 — clear but obvious gimmick
2 — integrated relational redesign

## C. Visual order
0 — cluttered
1 — reasonably organized
2 — strong field/figure/rhythm hierarchy

## D. Photographic credibility
0 — obvious synthetic fantasy
1 — mixed
2 — convincingly photographic

## E. Second-look depth
0 — immediately exhausted
1 — some ambiguity
2 — reveals a deeper abnormality after first reading

## F. Restraint
0 — too many effects
1 — slightly overworked
2 — only necessary operations remain

## G. New-world coherence
0 — edited photo
1 — partial new logic
2 — scene feels governed by a new reality

### Suggested threshold
- 12–14: strong
- 9–11: promising, revise
- 0–8: likely formulaic or incomplete
